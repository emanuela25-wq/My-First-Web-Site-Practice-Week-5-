Our Story: Building Indonesia's Global Leaders Since 2018

Since its establishment in 2018, Jakarta International University (JIU) has been committed to one clear purpose: to nurture a new generation of global leaders who will contribute to the future of Indonesia and make a positive impact on the world.

At JIU, education goes beyond academic knowledge. We prepare students with global perspectives, practical expertise, strong character, and the confidence to lead in an increasingly interconnected world. Through international collaboration with universities, educators, and industry partners across South Korea, Japan, China, India, the U.S.A., and beyond, students experience a truly multicultural and globally connected learning environment.

At the heart of JIU are our LIFE values — Love, Integrity, Faithfulness, and Excellence. These values shape not only how our students learn, but also who they become. We believe true global leaders are defined not only by what they achieve, but by the values they live by and the impact they create for others.

From Indonesia to the world, JIU empowers students to discover their potential, pursue their purpose, and become leaders who shape a better future.
